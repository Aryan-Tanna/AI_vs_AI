## Background, history, order below

Company-P, a real estate company, was the promoter of the residential project "Entity-EK" at Sector-79, Noida [F1]. By that date 806 of the project's 1080 units had been allotted to homebuyers, and 858 had been allotted by the insolvency commencement date; conveyance deeds had not been executed [F16, F17, F18]. On a Section 7 petition, the Adjudicating Authority admitted Company-P into CIRP on 29.10.2020, and Person-J was appointed Interim Resolution Professional [F2, F3, F4]. By an order dated 09.06.2021 Person-B replaced the Interim Resolution Professional, and a fresh Form-G was published on 30.06.2021 [F5, F6]. Bank-S had issued a notice under Section 13(2) of the SARFAESI Act dated 22.02.2022 [F21, EX-1]. Through the 16th to 19th CoC meetings Company-E raised objections on Section 30(2)(b) and the treatment of dissenting financial creditors [F24, F25, F26, F27, F28, EX-7, EX-8]. Company-K's plan was approved by the CoC on 10.07.2022 with 71.11% voting share; Company-E and Company-F voted for other plans [F29]. The plan provides Company-E Rs.70 Crores in four instalments from the 33rd to the 42nd month and preserves its rights against co-borrowers and other securities, and provides Company-F Rs.50,00,000/- [F36, F37, F38, EX-2]. The Resolution Professional valued Company-E's security interest at Rs.38,71,74,598.55/-, being 25.64% of the liquidation value of Rs.151,00,41,336/- [F22, F23]. Company-E and Company-F objected before the Adjudicating Authority, where Authority-U also intervened; Company-F's application was dismissed for non-prosecution on 04.07.2023 and it sought restoration [F30, F31, F32, F33, F34]. On 19.07.2023 the Adjudicating Authority approved the plan and rejected the objections [F35].

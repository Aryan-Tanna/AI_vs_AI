## Background, history, order below

Company-J obtained a facility of INR 500 crores from Company-S under a sanction letter dated 26.07.2011, secured by a demand promissory note and post-dated cheques, with no stated requirement of a guarantee from Company-Q [F1, F2, F3, EX-1]. Company-Q, as promoter, was required to give an undertaking to arrange for the infusion of funds, and an addendum and a Rupee Loan Agreement were executed the same day [F4, F5, F6, EX-2, EX-3]. On 27.07.2011 Company-Q executed a Deed of Undertaking, warranty, and indemnity whose Clause 2.2 concerns arranging funds for Company-J to meet its financial covenants [F7, F8, EX-4]. A supplementary agreement of 21.11.2011 amended the facility agreement and the security package [F9, EX-5]. Bank-Y filed an application under Section 7 of the Code concerning Company-J on 27.06.2017, which was admitted on 20.07.2017 [F10, F11]. Company-Q paid INR 38 crores to Company-S on 20.07.2017; the sides differ on why [F21, C1, EX-8]. On 17.04.2018 NCLT Kolkata approved the resolution plan of Company-D for Company-J, which was implemented, and Company-S issued a no due certificate to Company-J [F12, F13, F14, EX-6]. Company-S later claimed it had been allotted a reduced amount of shares and, on 30.06.2018, executed a Deed of Assignment of the alleged residual debt in favour of Company-K [F15, F16, EX-7]. Company-K filed an application under Section 7 of the Code before NCLT Cuttack, asserting a residual debt and a corporate guarantee by Company-Q [F17]. Company-K had also brought a proceeding to enforce the mortgage security given by Company-Q, in which Company-Q filed a pleading about the extent of its guarantee [F22, F23, C2, EX-10]. The NCLT dismissed the application on 24.06.2022, and the NCLAT dismissed the appeal against that order on 24.01.2024 [F18, F19, F20].

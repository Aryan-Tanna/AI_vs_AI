## Background, history, order below

Company-G, whose ex-directors are Person-L and Person-C, is in the business of manufacturing specialized steel wires [P2]. Person-K promised to invest Rs. 50 crores in Company-G, in return for 49% of its total shareholding [P3]. Person-K informed that funds for this investment would be transferred from his account and various firms, including Company-O, to Company-G [P3]. Company-O transferred Rs. 19,40,81,000 to Company-G [P3]. Person-K also transferred Rs. 25,00,000 through his sole proprietorship firm Company-V [F20]. Based on the promised investment, Company-G made plans for expansion and invested in infrastructure and other capital goods [F5]. Company-G's authorized share capital is Rs. 2 crores, and its paid-up share capital is Rs. 1,99,95,540 [F16, F17]. Company-O issued a demand notice under Section 8 of the IBC [F8]. Company-G responded to this notice on 2020-01-11, refuting the claim for repayment [F9]. Company-G filed a Civil Suit before the City Civil Court at Ahmedabad for declaration and damages, which is pending adjudication [F6, F7]. Company-O subsequently filed an application under Section 7 of the IBC against Company-G on 2020-02-04, alleging default in repayment of financial debt [F10]. The Adjudicating Authority admitted Company-O's Section 7 application via an order [F11]. An amount of Rs. 26,49,46,085 is outstanding on the loan given by Company-O to Company-G [F21].

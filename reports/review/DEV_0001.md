## Background, history, order below

The default in payment by the Corporate Debtor occurred on 10th June, 2014 [F1]. The Corporate Debtor sent a One Time Settlement proposal on 13th June, 2015, expressing its inability to revive the company and proposing to raise funds from investors to settle its liabilities [F5, EX-1]. Bank-Y approached the Debts Recovery Tribunal on 20th October, 2015 [F3]. The Corporate Debtor reiterated its One Time Settlement proposal on 6th November, 2015 [F6, EX-2]. Bank-Y filed an application under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 on 19th September, 2018 [F2].

- [ ] step 1 2015-10-20 Debts Recovery Tribunal: Bank-Y approached the Debts Recovery Tribunal on the basis of the default. (F3)
- [ ] step 2 2018-09-19 National Company Law Tribunal, Indore Bench at Ahmedabad, Court-1: Bank-Y filed an application under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016. (F2)
- [ ] step 3 2020-09-16 National Company Law Tribunal, Indore Bench at Ahmedabad, Court-1: The Adjudicating Authority rejected the Section 7 application as barred by limitation. (F4)

- [ ] order below: National Company Law Tribunal, Indore Bench at Ahmedabad, Court-1, REJECTED; The application under Section 7 was filed more than three years after the date of default (10th June, 2014) and was therefore barred by limitation.
  - The application under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 is rejected for being hit by limitation.

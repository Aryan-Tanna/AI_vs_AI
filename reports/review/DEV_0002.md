## Background, history, order below

The Corporate Debtor was admitted into CIRP on 16.06.2020 on an application by the Financial Creditor [F1]. A public announcement was made on 19.06.2020, and the Appellant filed its claim as an Operational Creditor on 01.08.2020 [F2]. The RP admitted a portion of the Appellant's claim [F2]. The Appellant's challenge to the rejection of its claim was initially dismissed but later directed to be considered during the hearing of the Resolution Plan [F3]. The Appellant filed an application to reject the Resolution Plan, which was dismissed by the Adjudicating Authority on 21.04.2022 [F4]. The Resolution Plan, which proposed nil payment to Operational Creditors while proposing payments to the sole Financial Creditor and CIRP costs, was approved by the CoC and the Adjudicating Authority [F5, F6, EX-1].

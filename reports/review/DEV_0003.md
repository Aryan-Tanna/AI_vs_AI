## Background, history, order below

The Corporate Debtor (Company-L) was declared an NPA by Bank-S on 28.02.2018 [F1]. Company-N advanced Rs. 2,500 Crores to the Corporate Debtor as interest-bearing Inter-Corporate Deposits, secured by a Deed of Hypothecation and an Indenture of Mortgage dated 07.03.2018 [F2, EX-1, EX-2]. In September 2018, Bank-S filed a Section 7 application against the Corporate Debtor [F3]. On 01.03.2019, Company-U obtained an assignment of the debt from Company-N via an Assignment Deed [F4, EX-3]. The Section 7 application was admitted on 15.01.2020 [F5]. Company-U filed its claim of Rs. 25,38,20,01,492/- as a Financial Creditor on 29.01.2020 [F6]. Following disputes regarding the admission of the claim, Company-U filed an Intervention Application, which was rejected by the Adjudicating Authority on 22.02.2022 [F7, F8]. The Assignment Deed was found to be insufficiently stamped [F9]. Authority-F also issued an interim order investigating circuitous transactions involving Company-U [F10, EX-4].

- [ ] step 1 2020-01-15 NCLT Ahmedabad Bench: Admission of Section 7 application filed by Bank-S against the Corporate Debtor (F5)
- [ ] step 2 2022-02-22 NCLT Ahmedabad Bench: Rejection of Intervention Application I.A. No. 02 of 2021 filed by Company-U (F8)

- [ ] order below: NCLT Ahmedabad Bench, REJECTED; The Adjudicating Authority held that the Assignment Deed dated 01.03.2019 was required to be registered under Section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908, and being insufficiently stamped under the Maharashtra Stamp Act, could not be looked into. It further held that the Appellant is a related party and disqualified under Section 21(2) of the IBC, and that a transaction done with malafide intention cannot give equivalent rights to those of unrelated financial creditors.
  - Reject the Intervention Application I.A. No. 02 of 2021 filed by the Appellant.
  - Hold that the Appellant is disqualified as a Financial Creditor as per the first proviso to Section 21(2) of the IBC as a related party.

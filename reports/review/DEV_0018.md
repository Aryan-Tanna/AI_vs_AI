## Background, history, order below

The CIRP of Company-I, the corporate debtor, was initiated by Company-A, and Person-W was appointed Interim Resolution Professional [F1]. The CIRP commenced on 04.07.2019 and the moratorium under Section 14 of the Code continued until the Resolution Plan was approved on 09.11.2021 [F2]. Claims were invited on 10.07.2019 with 22.07.2019 as the last date [F3, EX-10], and Person-P was appointed Resolution Professional on 09.10.2019 [F4]. Authority-R submitted a claim in Form B dated 24.09.2019 for Rs. 17.90 lakhs, which was reflected in the Information Memorandum [F5, F6, EX-1, EX-3]. The workmen and employees submitted claims for PF dues totalling Rs. 63.15 lakhs through their union or association, and Authority-R filed a revised claim of Rs. 52,14,739/- in Form-B dated 16.01.2020 [F7, F8, EX-2]. At the 5th CoC meeting on 02.03.2020 the Resolution Professional informed the resolution applicant that the PF liability was Rs. 63.15 lakhs, and the Resolution Plan was revised to provide that sum towards PF liabilities as full and final settlement [F9, F10, F11, EX-4]. The CoC approved the plan of Company-J on 20.11.2020 and the Adjudicating Authority approved it by order dated 09.11.2021 [F12, F13]. Company-J remitted Rs. 63.15 lakhs to the PF authorities, partly through ECR and partly by cheque dated 25.03.2022, and wrote on 25.03.2022 that the payment was a full and final settlement [F14, F15, EX-7]. Authority-R replied on 06.04.2022 that the payment had been adjusted under an order dated 06.04.2021 passed under Section 7A of the EPF & MP Act and that Rs. 1,13,482/- remained unpaid, and issued a notice dated 29.04.2022 demanding that sum [F16, F17, EX-5, EX-8, EX-9]. The Resolution Professional wrote on 13.05.2022 that no further amount was payable to Authority-R, and Company-J denied liability by letter dated 17.05.2022 [F18, F19, EX-10, EX-11]. Authority-R issued summons dated 18.05.2022 claiming Rs. 62,09,154/- for the period 01.04.2015 to 30.06.2019, a claim not made before plan approval and not reflected in the approved plan [F20, F21, F22, EX-12]. Company-J filed an application under Section 60(5) of the Code on 22.06.2022, which Authority-R opposed, and the Adjudicating Authority partly allowed it by order dated 09.10.2024 [F24, F25, F26, EX-13]. Both Company-J and Authority-R have appealed against that order [F29].

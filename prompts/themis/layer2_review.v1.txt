You check one argument by counsel in an insolvency case against the case record. You do not judge whether the argument is right in law or likely to win.

THE RECORD (the only facts in this case; nothing else is known)
$record

THE OPPONENT'S LAST SUBMISSION (empty if counsel speaks first)
<<<
$opponent
>>>

THE ARGUMENT
<<<
$argument
>>>

Return JSON {"fidelity_issues": [...], "opponent_points": [...]}.

fidelity_issues: each place where the argument states as fact something the record does not contain or contradicts.
- "NOT_IN_RECORD": a factual statement (an event, date, amount, act of a party) that no record item states or that contradicts a record item.
- "EXHIBIT_CONTENT_FABRICATED": a statement about what a document says or contains that goes beyond the exhibit's listed contents.
Each issue: {"kind", "quote": the argument's exact words, copied character for character, "record_ids": the record items concerned, "explanation", "confidence": 0 to 1}.
Do NOT report: legal argument, inferences counsel draws from recorded facts, characterisations ("this was an acknowledgment"), arguments about what the record does not show, or the other side's version of a contested fact stated as that side's version. When unsure, do not report it or give a low confidence.

opponent_points: the distinct points the opponent's last submission made (empty if there is none). For each: {"point": a short neutral summary, "addressed": true if the argument answers, rebuts or concedes it}.

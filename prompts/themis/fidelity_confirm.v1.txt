You double-check statements that a first reviewer flagged in one argument by counsel in an insolvency case. Each flagged statement would send the argument back to counsel, so be exact: confirm a flag only when the statement truly asserts a fact the record does not support.

THE RECORD (the parties and the only facts in this case; nothing else is known)
$record

THE FLAGGED STATEMENTS (each with the record items the first reviewer named and its reason)
$items

For each statement, return {"item": its number, "verdict": "CONFIRMED" or "NOT_CONFIRMED", "reason": one sentence}.

NOT_CONFIRMED when the statement is any of these:
- stated by a record item, in the same or other words;
- a direct inference from record items, including what follows from them in law;
- a party's own version of a contested fact, presented as that party's version;
- legal argument, characterisation, or an argument about what the record does not show.

CONFIRMED only when the statement presents as fact an event, date, amount, act or document content that no record item states, that cannot be inferred from them, or that contradicts a record item; or when it misstates a figure (including arithmetic on recorded amounts).

Return JSON {"checks": [...]} with one entry per statement.
